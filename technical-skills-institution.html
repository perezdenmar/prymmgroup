The Prymm Group’s Technical Skills Institution bridges the persistent gap between industrial demand and workforce supply. Anchored to TESDA’s National Certificate (NC) framework, TSI delivers technical-vocational training that moves graduates directly into industry-ready roles — operators, technicians, and service professionals who sustain the infrastructure Prymm deploys.

Three structured career tracks — Environmental Technology, Electromechanical Systems, and Digital Infrastructure — align precisely with the operational needs of our Industrial & Trade and Technology & Systems divisions. TSI graduates are not just employable; they are pre-qualified for roles within the Prymm Group ecosystem and its partner network.

Our Training Philosophy

Four Pillars of
TSI Training

Industry-First Curriculum

Every program is co-developed with industry partners and benchmarked against TESDA Training Regulations — ensuring graduates meet real-world competency standards from day one.

Hands-On Lab Training

70% of instruction time is practical, conducted on live equipment: thermal units, electrical panels, EV charging hardware, and network infrastructure — not simulations.

TESDA NC Certification

All completers are assessed for TESDA National Certificate qualifications — the recognised industry credential for technical-vocational graduates across the Philippines and ASEAN.

Employment Pipeline

TSI graduates enter a direct employment pipeline into Prymm Group operations and its industry partner network — shortening the gap between training completion and productive deployment.
