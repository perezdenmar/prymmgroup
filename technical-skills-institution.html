Education & Human Capital — Division Overview

Building the Workforce
That Runs the Future

The Prymm Group Technical Skills Institution (TSI) is a TESDA-accredited training center dedicated to producing work-ready, industry-aligned technical professionals. Every graduate is equipped to operate the machinery, systems, and infrastructure that The Prymm Group and its partners deploy across the Philippines and beyond.

Our programs are built around three skills tracks — Mechanical & Heavy Equipment, Electrical & EV Systems, and Environmental & Waste Technology — each directly mapped to the operational needs of our Industrial & Trade and Technology & Systems divisions.

Our Training Philosophy

Four Pillars of TSI

Industry-First Curriculum

Every module is co-designed with industry partners to ensure graduates meet real operational requirements — not just assessment standards.

Hands-On Learning

70% of training hours are practical, machine-on exercises conducted in fully equipped workshops that mirror real deployment environments.

TESDA NC Certification

All core courses lead to National Certificate (NC II or NC III) credentials recognised by TESDA and accepted by employers across ASEAN.

Employment Pathway

TSI graduates receive priority placement consideration across Prymm Group operations and partner deployments — closing the loop from training to employment.
