Developing the Operators
Who Run Every System We Build.

The Technical Skills Institution is the human capital engine of The Prymm Group. We train the technicians, operators, and safety specialists who sustain the physical and digital infrastructure our other divisions deploy.

Programmes are TESDA-registered and designed around practical, industry-relevant competencies — from heavy equipment operation to electrical systems and industrial safety. Graduates leave job-ready, not just certified.

Our Approach

Four Pillars of
Technical Education.

Industry-Led Curriculum

Every programme is co-designed with industry partners to ensure competencies match real-world job requirements — not just exam objectives.

All programmes are registered under TESDA, giving graduates nationally recognised qualifications that open doors across the Philippines and abroad.

Classroom theory is kept minimal. Students spend the majority of their time on actual equipment, in actual environments, solving actual problems.

Pipeline to Employment

Graduates have a direct pathway into Prymm Group projects and our partner network of contractors, LGUs, and private enterprises.
