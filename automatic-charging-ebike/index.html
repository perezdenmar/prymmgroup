Core Technical Architecture

How the System
Works

01
⚙

Dual-Motor
System Design

Two distinct motors work in tandem: a primary drive motor at the rear wheel propels the vehicle, while a dedicated generator motor integrated into the front wheel continuously captures rotational kinetic energy.

02
⚡

Kinetic Energy
Recovery

The system begins capturing rotational kinetic energy the moment the vehicle exceeds 7 km/h. Power generation reaches 100% efficiency during low-load states — coasting, downhill traversal, or cruising at steady speed.

03
▶

Manual Switching
Management

Power flow between motors and batteries is governed by a proprietary monorel switching system protected by international patents — enabling the rider to optimise between drive power and generation mode on demand.

04
⚛

Energy Conservation
Principle

The entire system is anchored in the physics of mechanical energy conservation — K₁ + U₁ = K₂ + U₂ — ensuring that kinetic and potential energy are continuously cycled back into usable electrical charge rather than lost as heat.

◆
Japan Patent No. 7799024

The dual-motor drivetrain and kinetic energy recovery system are fully protected under Japan Patent No. 7799024, with Taiwan and international PCT patents obtained — securing exclusive manufacturing and licensing rights globally.

✓
Zero CO₂ Emissions

The Plus Ultra produces zero CO₂ emissions across its operational lifecycle — no combustion, no grid-sourced electricity required. A fully clean mobility solution for dense urban and rural environments alike.

★
Massive Addressable Market
