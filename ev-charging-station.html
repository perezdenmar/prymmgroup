DOE-Accredited
EVCS Provider

Prymm Group has officially been accredited by the Department of Energy (DOE) as an authorized Electric Vehicle Charging Station (EVCS) Provider-Operator, receiving Certificate of Accreditation Number DOE-EUMB-ANA-20260506001.

Granted under the Electric Vehicle Industry Development Act (EVIDA) framework, this accreditation officially permits Prymm to construct, operate, and manage public EV charging infrastructure and collect retail charging fees from EV drivers. The certification validates that Prymm’s charging technologies and operational workflows comply with strict national safety, engineering, and data security standards.

This milestone positions Prymm as a trusted partner in driving the country’s transition toward sustainable electromobility.
