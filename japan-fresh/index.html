Livestock Division

Premium
Wagyu.

We source exclusively A-5 Grade Kuroge Wagyu (Japanese Black) — strictly female cattle — from verified farm partners across Japan. Each cut is backed by an official certificate of authenticity verifying age, processing date, and prestigious regional origin.

  • A-5 Grade, Strictly Female

    Only the highest graded Kuroge Wagyu passes our selection. Female cattle produce consistently superior marbling scores.

  • Full Traceability

    Individual identification management on every animal ensures complete, verifiable traceability from farm to table.

  • Certificates of Authenticity

    Official documentation accompanies every shipment, verifying the cut’s age, processing date, and verified origin.

Global Market Strategy

Halal
Certified.

To serve the global Muslim demographic, Prymm Farm partners exclusively with certified Halal beef processing plants and slaughterhouses in Japan. Every step of the processing chain adheres strictly to Islamic dietary law — enabling access to markets across Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

Certified Slaughter

All beef is processed at facilities certified under recognized Halal standards, ensuring full compliance from slaughter to packaging.

Export-Ready

Our processing and certification system is purpose-built for international export to Muslim-majority markets and global premium retailers.

Strategic Market Access

Targeting Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia — unlocking a massive global demographic for premium Japanese beef.
