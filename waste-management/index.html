How It
Works.

Sanitary landfills across the country are rapidly overflowing and generating massive greenhouse gases. The KGV Series solves this through controlled thermal incineration — destroying waste at temperatures exceeding 800°C while meeting the strictest clean air standards.

95% waste volume reduction
slashing landfill dependency and extending site lifespans
Fuel-less forced airflow
fans sustain ultra-high heat combustion, keeping operating costs low
Secondary burner failsafe
instantly ignites at the exhaust to eliminate toxic fumes before venting
Hospital-grade sterilization
destroys biohazards, syringes, and medical waste on-site
Clean Air Act compliant
releases harmless clean steam, not toxic black smoke
